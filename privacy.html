Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

In short: sorting happens in your browser, so the Instagram profiles and reels you sort never reach our servers. We only receive your Google sign-in details, your plan and payments, and the reels you choose to transcribe. No analytics, no ads, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Sortcut is a product of Current Culture, a business run by its proprietor, Kartik, in Sirsa, Haryana, India. "We" and "us" mean Current Culture. This policy covers sortcut.co, the account dashboard at accounts.sortcut.co and the Sortcut browser extension. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, Google account ID and the link to your Google profile picture. You only sign in to buy Pro or to export to Google Sheets. Sorting on the Free plan needs no account.
  • Your plan and usage: your plan, billing dates, subscription status, and how many transcriptions you've used in each billing period.
  • Payment records: Razorpay's payment and subscription IDs, amounts, dates, and the tax invoices and credit notes we issue to you.
  • Business details, if you add them: your GSTIN, your business's legal name and its billing address, so we can print them on your tax invoices. These are optional.
  • Email delivery records: whether each email we sent you was delivered or bounced.
  • Messages you send us at support@sortcut.co.

3. What we never collect

  • The Instagram profiles and reels you sort. Sorting runs in your browser. The profiles you open, the reels you sort, their numbers and the files you export never reach our servers.
  • Your Instagram password. Sortcut works in your own browser, where you're already logged in to Instagram.
  • Your card or UPI details. Razorpay handles them (see section 6).
  • Analytics or tracking. There are no analytics, ad trackers or tracking pixels on sortcut.co, on accounts.sortcut.co or in the extension.

The extension keeps its settings, your sign-in and your latest sort in your browser's own storage, on your computer.

4. Transcription

Transcription is part of Pro, and happens only when you click Transcribe on a reel:

  • The extension downloads the reel's audio from Instagram in your browser and uploads it to our server, along with the reel's short code (the ID in its link). If Instagram doesn't offer the audio on its own, the extension uploads the reel's video file instead.
  • Our server sends the audio to Groq, our transcription provider, which turns the speech into text. For Hindi, Groq also writes the text out in Latin script (Hinglish).
  • We delete the audio as soon as the transcript is ready. A storage rule removes anything left behind within 1 day.
  • We keep the transcript for 30 days, so that opening the same reel again doesn't use up another transcription. After that it's deleted.

5. Anonymous error reports (optional)

If a sort fails in a way that may mean Instagram changed something, the extension may ask whether you want to send us an anonymous error report. Nothing is sent unless you click to send it.

A report contains only: the type of error, the version of the extension and of its sorting rules, your browser and its version, and counts from the sort (such as how many reels were found, how many times the page scrolled and how long it took). It contains no profile, reel, link, account, email address or IP address, and it's sent without your sign-in. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

6. Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. You enter your card or UPI details in Razorpay's checkout, and we never see or store them. We give Razorpay your name and email address to fill in the checkout for you.

We keep Razorpay's payment and subscription IDs, the amounts and dates you paid, and the tax invoices and credit notes we issue. Razorpay may also email you directly about your payments and AutoPay. Razorpay's own privacy policy covers the data it holds.

7. Tax records

GST law requires us to keep our tax invoices and credit notes. We keep them, with the buyer details printed on them (your name and email address, and your GSTIN, business name and billing address if you added them), for as long as GST law requires. This applies even after you delete your account.

8. Emails

We only email you:

  • a welcome email when you first sign in,
  • a receipt with your tax invoice after each payment, and a credit note if we refund you,
  • emails about your account, such as important changes to this policy, and replies when you write to us.

App emails are sent through Amazon SES. We never send marketing emails or newsletters. If an email to you bounces, we stop sending to that address.

9. Google Sheets export

Exporting to Google Sheets asks Google for one permission, drive.file. It lets Sortcut create files in your Google Drive and see only the files Sortcut created, and nothing else in your Drive. The sheet goes from your browser straight to your own Drive. It never passes through our servers, and the Drive permission stays in your browser.

Exporting to Sheets also signs you in to Sortcut, so we receive the Google sign-in details in section 2. CSV, JSON and Excel exports are made in your browser and need no sign-in.

Sortcut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Where your data is stored, and who processes it

  • Amazon Web Services, in Sydney, Australia: our servers, our database, uploaded audio until it's deleted, and our app emails (Amazon SES).
  • Groq: transcription (section 4).
  • Razorpay: payments (section 6).
  • Google: sign-in, and our support mailbox.
  • Vercel: hosts sortcut.co and accounts.sortcut.co.

This means your data is stored and processed outside India. We don't sell or rent personal data. We share it with government authorities only when the law requires it.

11. Cookies

  • One sign-in cookie, for accounts.sortcut.co only. When you sign in to the account dashboard, our server sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. Scripts on the page can't read it, and it's sent only to our own server.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, anywhere. sortcut.co sets no cookies at all.
  • On the checkout page only, Razorpay's payment window may set its own cookies to process your payment.
  • sortcut.co loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

12. How long we keep it

  • Account, plan and usage details: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: 60 days.
  • Uploaded audio: deleted when the transcript is ready, and within 1 day at most.
  • Transcripts: 30 days. Transcription job records (the reel's code and the job's status): 7 days. Error reports: 90 days. Email delivery records: 1 year. Server logs: up to 14 days.
  • Payment records, invoices and credit notes: for as long as GST law requires (section 7).

Our database keeps rolling backups for 35 days, so deleted data can stay in a backup for up to 35 days before it's gone for good.

13. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent. Email support@sortcut.co from the address on your account. When you delete your account, we cancel any subscription, erase your data and keep only the tax records in section 7. We respond within 30 days.

Grievance contact: Kartik Chaudhry, support@sortcut.co. See Contact us for our full address and phone number. If we don't resolve your complaint, you can take it to the Data Protection Board of India.

14. Security

All data is encrypted in transit, and uploaded audio is encrypted at rest. Sign-in tokens are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, so a copy of our database can't be used to sign in as you. Only the people who need access to run Sortcut have it.

15. Children

Sortcut isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

16. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll email account holders before the change takes effect.

17. Business details

Current Culture (proprietor: Kartik), Sirsa, Haryana, India. Email: support@sortcut.co. See Contact us for our full address and phone number.
