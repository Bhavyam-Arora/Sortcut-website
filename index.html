Features

Everything you need to find your next winning reel.

The outlier score

Big numbers can mislead. 50K views is a bad day for a big account and a breakout for a small one. Sortcut divides each reel's views by that profile's median, so you see what beat the account's own normal.

Six ways to sort

Views, likes and comments, each with an outlier version. Ties go to the newest post.

Reel to text in one click

Every reel gets a Transcribe button. Hindi comes back in Hinglish, the way people actually type it.
