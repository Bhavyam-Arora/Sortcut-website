Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

Sortcut is a digital subscription. Nothing physical is shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

How you get the service

  1. Install the Sortcut extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Sign in with your Google account and pay through Razorpay.
  3. Pro features unlock in the extension immediately once payment is confirmed, usually within a minute.

A payment confirmation and invoice are sent to your email.

If access doesn't arrive

If Pro isn't active within 30 minutes of a successful payment, sign out of the extension and back in. If that doesn't fix it, email support@sortcut.co with your payment reference. We'll activate your account by hand within 1 business day, or refund you in full.

Where we deliver

The service is available to customers in India paying in INR.
